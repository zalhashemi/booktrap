# Design System: BookTrap

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Annotated Paperback"**

BookTrap is a well-loved paperback that its reader has marked up: coloured sticky page-tabs, a lime highlighter stroke across a fresh find, pencil notes in the margin. The shelf is a reading list annotated the way BookTok readers annotate books. It rejects both the generic grey list app and the museum-piece dark-academia library: playful, rounded and warm, never solemn.

Light is the default: a washed Honeydew ground with near-white cards and deep-grape ink. The dark theme is a softer night version on deep Vintage Grape, never near-black. Depth is soft and low (hairline inset rules plus a diffuse drop), a faint paper grain sits over everything, and the type does the hierarchy through scale contrast rather than ornament. On wide screens the page sits inside a drawn reading room (arched window, candle on a book stack with a bookworm, hanging lamp, bookcase with ladder) that changes from day to night with the theme.

Density is relaxed and mobile-first: one 1080px column, 44px minimum targets, a floating tab bar on phones and a pill nav in the header on desktop.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Page-tabs are the signature control: filter tabs standing on a teal page edge, and state tabs peeking from every cover's top edge.
- Teal is the one colour that means "act"; lime appears only for New.
- Young Serif for headlines and book titles, Nunito for every control, Kalam for sparing margin notes.
- Pills for anything you press, 20px cards, 14px fields, 5px covers.
- Status is never colour alone: every state carries a word or a glyph.
- Motion is small and physical: a press-in on tap, a tab dropping onto a cover, a highlighter sweeping in once.

## Colors

Five user-chosen colours (Dusty Lavender, Vintage Grape, Slate Grey, Muted Teal, Honeydew) plus one reserved lime; every other value is a tint or shade of those five, and components use semantic role tokens only, never raw hex.

### Primary
- **Muted Teal** (accent / action): the one colour that means "act": primary buttons, active nav pills, active page-tabs (All, Want to read), the page edge under the tab row, selection fills, the text caret. Teal as text uses the deepened **Teal Ink** (accent-ink), which is also the success colour and, in light, the focus ring.

### Secondary
- **Dusty Lavender** (note / note-ink): margin notes, gentle alerts, private-post locks, destructive-but-quiet actions (Delete in the selection bar and book sheet), the storage warning outline.
- **Slate Grey** (reading / tag-ink): the Reading state and its tabs, plus tags and "Seen in N posts" source lines.

### Tertiary
- **Honeydew** (read): the Read state fill. In dark it is also the body text colour.
- **New Lime** (#C6E04A): reserved. The highlighter stroke behind a new title, the New chip, the New filter tab when pulled up, and the New cover tab. Nothing else.

### Neutral
- **Washed Honeydew Ground** (ground, light) / **Deep Grape Ground** (dark-ground): the page.
- **Raised Ground** (ground-raised): sheets, the phone tab bar, dialogs.
- **Card White** / **Lifted Grape** (surface): cards, page-tabs at rest, quiet buttons.
- **Sunk Page** (surface-sunk): inputs, set slightly into the card like a page beneath it.
- **Strong Surface** (surface-strong): toasts, disabled primary, scrollbar thumbs, progress tracks.
- **Hairline Rule** (rule): 1.5px inset outlines on cards, quiet buttons, fields.
- **Grape Ink** (text, light) with muted and faint steps; **Honeydew Ink** (dark-text) with lifted slate steps. All text steps hold 4.5:1 or better on their ground.
- **Bookcloth** (cloth-1 to cloth-4): the four deepened palette cloths for coverless books, identical in both themes, lettered in on-cloth.
- **Grape Scrim** (scrim): the dimmed backdrop behind every modal sheet and dialog, a translucent deep grape rather than black.
- **Wordmark Deboss** (wordmark-deboss): a 1px hairline text-shadow under the teal wordmark, dark theme only; the light theme drops it.

### Named Rules
**The One Action Colour Rule.** Teal means "act". Nothing decorative wears a teal fill except the page edge and the reading room's flame and leaves.

**The Reserved Lime Rule.** Lime is for New and nothing else: highlighter, New chip, New tab, New cover tab. It is the same value in both themes; dark mode shows the resting New tab as a lime outline on the card colour because a lime tint goes olive on grape.

**The Ink-On-Tab Rule.** Every tab and state fill (teal, slate, honeydew, lime) carries the same dark ink (on-tab), never white.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Young Serif (with Georgia, serif)
**Body Font:** Nunito Variable (with ui-rounded, system-ui, sans-serif)
**Annotation Font:** Kalam (with Segoe Print, cursive)

All three are self-hosted via fontsource; the PDF export embeds the same faces as WOFF with per-glyph fallback stacks (Young Serif, Young Serif Ext, then Nunito Cyrillic and Vietnamese).

**Character:** A chunky, friendly serif that reads like a paperback's title page, set against a rounded sans that makes every control feel soft to press, with a pencil hand reserved for the reader's own asides.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (Young Serif 400, line-height 1.05 to 1.08): one page headline per screen. Every page headline steps up to 3.1rem (display) from 768px. On phones each page sets its own step within a 2 to 2.4rem band: Settings 2.4rem (display-phone-lg), Add 2.3rem (display-phone), Shelf 2.2rem (display-phone-sm), the 404 page 2.15rem at every width (display-compact), and the full-screen connection states 2rem (display-state). Balanced wrapping.
- **Headline** (Young Serif 400, 1.35 to 1.7rem): sheet and section headings. Export sheet title 1.7rem (headline-lg), book sheet title 1.55rem (headline-sheet), Settings sections and empty states 1.5rem (headline), the merge dialog 1.4rem (headline-sm), the In progress heading beside the paste card 1.35rem (headline-xs).
- **Wordmark** (Young Serif 400, 1.45rem on phones, 1.6rem from 768px): the teal header wordmark. The 1.6rem step also sets the "Opening your shelf" line on the waking-server screen.
- **Title** (Young Serif 400, snug leading): book titles, always in the display face. 1.12rem in list rows (title), 0.92rem under grid covers (title-grid).
- **Body** (Nunito 400, 1rem, line-height 1.55): copy and field text; supporting lines at 1.05rem in text-muted, capped near 48ch; secondary text 0.875rem.
- **Label** (Nunito 700, 0.85 to 0.98rem): controls. Buttons 0.98rem (label-lg); page-tabs, nav pills and field labels 0.92rem (label); segmented toggles and export row buttons 0.88rem (label-sm); compact pills (view toggle, link chips, small export actions, Mark all as seen) 0.85rem (label-xs).
- **Micro** (Nunito 700 to 800, 0.7 to 0.8rem): small metadata, never body copy. Tags, sources and picker labels 0.8rem (micro); New and status chips and job stage badges 0.78rem (micro-chip); phone tab-bar labels and the status word under grid covers 0.75rem (micro-nav); 0.7rem (micro-count) only for the numeric count badge on the phone filter button, whose count is also in the button's accessible name. Counts use tabular figures.
- **Marginalia** (Kalam 400, 0.95 to 1.1rem, line-height 1.3, rotated -1.5deg, note-ink): short pencil asides beside a label or under a heading.

### Named Rules
**The Titles-Are-Serif Rule.** Headlines and book titles are Young Serif; every control, label and number is Nunito. A button is never serif and a book title is never sans.

**The Micro Floor Rule.** Nothing smaller than 0.75rem carries words; 0.7rem is for a single count in a badge that duplicates an accessible name.

**The Pencil Rule.** Kalam is sparing and decorative: a margin note is never the only carrier of something you must read to act, and it is aria-hidden when it duplicates visible text.

## Layout

One centred column (max 1080px) with 16px side gutters on phones and 32px from 768px. The header is 64px (72px from 768px) and sticky on the ground colour. Phones get a floating three-item tab bar inset 12px from the edges above the safe area, so main content reserves 144px of bottom padding; from 768px the nav moves into the header as a pill group.

The Add screen is a single column on phones and two columns from 1024px (paste card up to 620px, progress beside it). Cards pad 16px on phones and 24px from 768px. The shelf shows covers at one 2:3 ratio on a shared baseline (grid view) or 64px covers in rows (list view). On phones the page-tab row scrolls horizontally with a right-edge fade while more tabs are off screen; filter and select buttons sit at the end of that row.

The reading room is breakpoint-driven: from 1240px two decorative wings flank the column (fixed, behind content, each a size container so pieces appear only when they fit: window from about 150px-wide wings, bookcase from 230px, both dropped on screens under 560px tall). From 768 to 1239px a bookcase footer runs below the content. Below 1240px a short reading-room strip (max 480px wide) sits above the Add headline.

## Elevation & Depth

Hybrid and low. Surfaces separate by tone (ground, raised ground, card, sunk field) and by a 1.5px inset hairline in the rule colour; resting cards and primary buttons add one soft, diffuse drop. Floating elements (phone tab bar, selection bar) use the larger lift. A faint fractal paper grain (3.5% light, 5% dark) sits fixed over the whole page. Covers take the soft drop plus a dark sheen along the spine edge so they read as books, not tiles.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Soft** (`box-shadow: 0 10px 22px -14px rgb(43 39 51 / 0.3)`; dark `0 10px 24px -14px rgb(12 8 20 / 0.7)`): paste card, primary buttons, covers.
- **Lift** (`box-shadow: 0 18px 34px -18px rgb(43 39 51 / 0.4)`; dark `0 18px 36px -18px rgb(12 8 20 / 0.8)`): everything that floats or overlays: phone tab bar, selection bar, sheets, the merge dialog, toasts (dialog and toast add a 1px rule-accent inset).
- **Wordmark deboss** (`text-shadow: 0 1px 0 rgb(0 0 0 / 0.35)`): the dark-theme wordmark only.
- **Hairline** (`box-shadow: inset 0 0 0 1.5px var(--rule)`): the outline on cards, quiet buttons, toggles; hover swaps to rule-accent.
- **Focus halo** (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 4px var(--rule-accent)`): fields on focus.

### Named Rules
**The Hairline-Not-Border Rule.** Containers are outlined with a 1.5px inset shadow, not a CSS border, so the outline never changes the box size. Fields are the exception (a real 1.5px border that turns teal on focus).

## Shapes

One shape system: pills for anything you press (buttons, nav, toggles, chips, search, selects, toasts), soft 20px cards, gentler 14px fields and icon tiles, book-like 5px covers. Floating bars round at 22px with 16px inner items (16px is also the radius for items nested inside a sheet or card: merge options, the backup code); the merge dialog rounds at 24px; sheets round their leading corners at 26px. Page-tabs are their own silhouette: 12px top corners, 3px bottom corners (`border-radius: 12px 12px 3px 3px`), so they read as paper tabs standing on an edge. Cover tabs round only their top corners (6px). The New highlighter is a drawn marker shape with a tilted top and hand-cut ends, never a rectangle.

## Components

### Buttons
Soft, pressable pills with one shared physical response.
- **Shape:** full pill (999px), 48px tall (56px for the main Catch books submit), 24px side padding, Nunito 700.
- **Primary:** teal fill, dark ink, a 1px inner top highlight plus the soft drop. Disabled turns strong-surface with faint text and no shadow.
- **Quiet:** card fill with a hairline; hover warms the hairline to rule-accent.
- **Ghost:** muted text, card fill on hover.
- **Hover / Focus / Press:** colour transitions over 200ms on the snappy curve; every control scales to 0.97 on press; focus is a 2px focus-colour outline at 3px offset.

### Chips
- **New chip:** lime pill, dark ink, 0.78rem extra-bold.
- **Status chips:** Reading in slate, Read in honeydew, both pills with dark ink and the status word. Want to read shows no chip.
- **Tags and sources:** bold slate text (#tag, "Seen in N posts"), no fill.
- **Link chips (Add):** pill with a hairline, kind in bold plus @handle in muted; ignored links are struck through.

### Cards / Containers
- **Corner Style:** 20px, for cards, job progress cards and book list rows (which fill with the card colour on hover and select).
- **Background:** surface on the ground.
- **Shadow Strategy:** hairline inset; the paste card adds the soft drop.
- **Internal Padding:** 16px phones, 24px from 768px.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** sunk-page fill, 1.5px rule border, 14px radius, 46px minimum, 16px text (prevents iOS zoom). Search fields and selects become pills; selects draw their own chevron.
- **Focus:** border turns teal and a 4px rule-accent halo appears. Hover warms the border.
- **Placeholder:** its own token, AA on the sunk fill in both themes.

### Navigation
- **Desktop:** a pill group in the header (card fill, hairline), 44px pill items with a Phosphor icon and bold label; active is a teal fill with dark ink and a filled icon.
- **Phone:** a floating 64px tab bar (raised ground, 22px radius, lift shadow) with three stacked icon-and-label items; active is a teal 16px-radius fill.

### Page-Tabs (signature)
Sticky paper tabs on a 3px teal page edge. Each tab wears the colour of the state it stands for: a soft tint of it at rest, full colour when pulled up (All and Want to read in teal, New in lime, Reading in slate, Read in honeydew), always with its word and a tabular count. At rest a tab sits 4px low, rises to 1px on hover and to 0 when pressed, over 220ms on the snappy curve. The pale Read tab and the light-theme New tab carry an ink edge so they hold 3:1 on the pale ground.

### Cover Tabs and Plates (signature)
Every cover carries its state as small tabs peeking 14px above its top-right edge: lime with a sparkle glyph for New, slate with an open-book glyph for Reading, honeydew with a check for Read, each with an inset edge. A newly set tab drops onto the cover once (0.5s, ease-out-expo). Coverless books get a bookcloth plate: one of four cloths and one of three bindings (gilt rules, head-and-tail bands, label panel) picked by title hash, the title in Young Serif sized to the plate and the author in Kalam.

### New Highlighter (signature)
A new book's title sits on a lime marker stroke (a drawn SVG shape, cloned across line breaks) that sweeps in once per book per visit (0.7s). The PDF export renders the same idea as a flat lime background.

### Sheets, Dialogs and Toasts
- **Sheet:** native modal dialog on raised ground over the grape scrim, with the lift shadow; rises from the bottom on phones (full height minus 24px, 26px top corners) and slides in from the right at 480px wide from 768px. Close buttons are 44px ghost pills.
- **Segmented toggles** (book status, export scope): a pill track with a hairline, 44px pill segments, the active segment teal.
- **Toast:** strong-surface pill with the lift shadow and a 1px rule-accent hairline, an optional teal action pill; enters with a short rise.

### Job Progress
20px cards with a hairline (the same card radius as everywhere else): author handle and platform, a pill stage badge, and a four-segment pill progress track that fills teal. Success lines use teal ink with a filled check; problems use note-ink with a warning glyph and a next step.

### Reading Room
Decoration only (aria-hidden, no pointer events), painted entirely from the deco tokens in a day and a night version. Its motion is three slow loops (candle flicker, glow breathing, dust drifting through the lamplight) using transform and opacity only, all removed under reduced motion while the static scene stays.

### Print (PDF reading list)
A paper variant of the light theme: card-white page, grape ink, a 7px teal page edge down the left, Young Serif title with a Kalam margin note, status groups headed over a 1.5px teal rule with handwritten counts, covers at one size with the same bookcloth picks as the app.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** use the semantic role tokens (accent, note, reading, read, surface) in components, never a palette hex.
- **Do** pair every status colour with its word or glyph: tab labels, status chips, cover-tab glyphs.
- **Do** keep every interactive target at 44px or more, including icon-only buttons and nav pills.
- **Do** keep ink dark (on-tab) on every teal, slate, honeydew and lime fill.
- **Do** give every entrance and loop a reduced-motion path; entrances are skipped entirely, never left at a first-frame offset.
- **Do** keep covers at one 2:3 ratio on a shared baseline, with the bookcloth plate always underneath so a slow cover never leaves a blank.
- **Do** keep the dark theme on deep grape (#2b2733) and check both themes for AA contrast.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use lime for anything but New.
- **Don't** use teal fills for decoration on interface surfaces; teal means act.
- **Don't** set controls in Young Serif or book titles in Nunito.
- **Don't** put information you need to act on only in Kalam margin notes.
- **Don't** use near-black for the dark ground or white text on state fills.
- **Don't** square off a pressable control; anything you press is a pill.
- **Don't** let the reading room catch pointer events, carry meaning, or animate under reduced motion.
